AnaLog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AnaLog keeps your film log on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends none of your data to the developer or to anyone else.

What AnaLog stores, and where

Everything you enter — rolls, frames, exposures, cameras, lenses, scans, notes and any GPS coordinates you attach — is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, synced to a server, or shared. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — used only by the built-in light meter, which reads the brightness of the scene live. No photograph is captured, saved or transmitted; the image is measured and discarded.
  • Location — used to tag a frame with the coordinates of where it was shot, when you choose to do so. The coordinates are saved with that frame on your device. Location is only read while you are using the app.
  • Photo library — used to save scans to your library, and to let you pick scans from it to attach to a roll.

Each of these is optional. iOS asks before granting any of them, and AnaLog works without them — you lose only the feature that needs the permission.

The one network request

AnaLog downloads its film stock catalog from alekperez.github.io, so that new film stocks can appear without an app update. This is a plain request for a static file. It carries no account, no device identifier and nothing about you or your rolls.

The file is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's servers see the request the same way any website sees a visit — including the IP address it came from. That is the extent of it, and it is the only network request the app makes.

Exporting and backups

AnaLog can export your roll metadata for use in Lightroom or Capture One, and can write a backup file of your whole log. Both go wherever you send them — the Files app, AirDrop, your Mac — and never to the developer.

Worth knowing: an export includes the GPS coordinates of any frame that has them. That is the point of the feature, but it means location data can leave your device inside a file you share. It only ever leaves because you exported it.

What AnaLog does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is none to sell or share.

Children's privacy

AnaLog collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no content requiring additional safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe, this page changes first. The date at the top is when it was last revised.
