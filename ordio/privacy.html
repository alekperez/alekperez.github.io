Privacy Policy for Ordio

Last updated: August 2026

Ordio is a daily word game designed to be simple, fun, and privacy-friendly.

Information We Collect

Ordio does not collect or store any personal data on our own servers.

All game data, such as progress, streaks, and statistics, is stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Ordio offers an optional Premium subscription. All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Word Lists

Ordio downloads its daily word lists from our own server (GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this transmits standard technical information such as your IP address to deliver the content, but Ordio does not use it to identify or track you, and no personal data is sent.

Analytics

Ordio does not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Third-Party Services

Ordio uses Apple (App Store and StoreKit) to process optional purchases and GitHub Pages to host the word lists it downloads. Neither is used to track you, and Ordio integrates no advertising or analytics SDKs.
